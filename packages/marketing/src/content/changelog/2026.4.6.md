This release adds drag & drop file support, Vim and Emacs keybindings for the query editor, and fixes a bug related to unsharing projects.

---

## Drag & Drop Files

**Import Data by Dropping Files** — Drag and drop Parquet, CSV, JSON, Excel, or DuckDB files directly into the app to open them in an in-memory DuckDB instance, letting you quickly query and explore your data. A visual overlay guides you through the process.

---

## Vim & Emacs Keybindings

**Editor Keybinding Modes** — Switch the query editor to Vim or Emacs keybindings from the settings panel. Your preference is persisted across sessions.

---

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where connections were incorrectly deleted when unsharing a project
