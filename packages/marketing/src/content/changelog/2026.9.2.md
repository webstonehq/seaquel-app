This is a hotfix for a bug introduced in 2026.9.1. If you're on the desktop app, update to this release.

---

## Bug Fixes

- **Fixed the desktop app failing to load** — After 2026.9.1, every page in the desktop app rendered a blank "500 Internal Error" screen. The account sign-in support added for Seaquel Cloud was being initialized on startup even in the desktop build, where it doesn't apply, and the failure stopped the app from loading at all. It's now set up only on the web, where it's actually used.
